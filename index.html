01 / THE BRIEF

Building a complete accounting cycle

This project simulates the October 2026 month end close for Bluewater Office Supplies Pty Ltd, a fictional Australian business. The aim was to connect core accounting concepts with a structured accounting workflow, including transaction recording, reconciliation, adjusting entries and reporting.

02 / WORKFLOW

How the accounting process works

STEP 01

Record transactions

Document sales, inventory purchases, capital contributions, operating expenses and customer receipts.

STEP 02

Prepare journal entries

Apply double entry principles to identify the debit and credit for each transaction.

STEP 03

Post to the ledger

Group entries by account and calculate balances for the trial balance.

STEP 04

Reconcile cash

Investigate timing differences between the bank statement and the accounting cash book.

STEP 05

Make adjustments

Account for accruals, prepayments and depreciation at the reporting date.

STEP 06

Report performance

Prepare financial statements and assess profitability, liquidity and balance sheet integrity.

What was reconciled?

The accounting exercise considers outstanding cheques and deposits in transit to explain differences between bank and book records.

A bank reconciliation is not simply matching two numbers. It identifies the reasons for differences and confirms the adjusted balances agree.

Interpreting the figures

Gross margin: A$8,100,000 gross profit ÷ A$13,500,000 revenue = 60.0%.

Net margin: A$2,850,000 profit before tax ÷ A$13,500,000 revenue = 21.1%.

These ratios illustrate the difference between profitability after direct product costs and profitability after operating expenses.

A single month of fictional results is not sufficient to infer sustainable profitability or benchmark industry performance.
