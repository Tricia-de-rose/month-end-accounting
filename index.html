A forward contract can lock in the Australian dollar cost of a known US dollar obligation. It reduces uncertainty but may result in a higher cost if the Australian dollar strengthens before payment.

06 / Findings & Recommendation

What the analysis tells us

BASE CASE CONCLUSION

Proceed to detailed due diligence

At the base case discount rate, the expansion generates a positive NPV and an IRR above the cost of capital. This suggests the project may create value, subject to validation of the cash flow assumptions, implementation costs and operational risks.

Given the material US dollar equipment purchase, management should also consider a forward hedge to improve certainty over the Australian dollar purchase cost. The hedge protects against adverse currency movements but removes the benefit of favourable moves.

Methodology & Assumptions

Financial performance is presented using a fictional five year history. Investment appraisal applies discounted cash flow analysis, NPV, IRR and simple payback. WACC uses a stylised capital structure and tax shield. Foreign exchange risk is assessed through hypothetical forward versus unhedged outcomes.
